# AFGS v1.0 — Limitations

**Source:** Specification §20.4; publication paper §12.

## Documented limitations

| ID | Source limitation | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KL-R5 | A normative executable Conformance Test Profile is not yet completed. | The specification alone does not demonstrate SC2/SC3; a canonical executable fixture suite is not supplied. |
| KL-R6 | A universal data-classification or tenancy taxonomy is not standardized. | Isolation controls must bind to an approved organizational data-governance model; cross-tenant protection cannot be inferred from the document alone. |

These limitations do not permit weakening other controls or claiming a higher conformance level. Selected source passages are reproduced in [the technical excerpts](../provenance/AFGS-v1.0-source-excerpts.txt).

## Evidence limits

The paper distinguishes internal AI-assisted review from external human validation. It does not report measured false-allow/false-block rates, routing precision/recall, latency, cost or human-review burden. A complete architecture description is not a running governance system.

The source's **SC0 / SC1-capable** statement is not evidence of deployed SC1 compliance, SC2/SC3 enforcement or external certification. Evaluation targets remain distinct from measured results.
